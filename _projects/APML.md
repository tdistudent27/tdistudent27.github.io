## Overview

This work investigates whether machine learning can enhance cross-sectional return predictability in asset pricing.  
It builds directly on the seminal framework of <a href="https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/33/5/2223/5758276" target="_blank">Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2020)</a>, who demonstrated that nonlinear models such as neural networks can significantly improve predictive accuracy compared to traditional linear methods.

The research question is whether their results remain valid when excluding the early, less efficient period of their sample — testing the **temporal stability** and **economic relevance** of machine learning in modern financial markets.

---

## Research Motivation

- **Predicting stock returns** remains one of the most challenging and central problems in empirical finance.  
- Advances in machine learning provide new tools to capture complex, nonlinear relationships among firm characteristics.  
- This thesis evaluates whether such methods continue to add value in the current market environment, characterized by reduced predictability and structural changes in trading behavior.
