## Introduction
Impulse Response Functions (IRFs) are widely used in macroeconometrics to trace the dynamic effects of shocks. Traditionally estimated with Structural VARs (SVARs), since Jordà (2005) Local Projections (LPs) have become a popular alternative. Building on the results of Plagborg-Møller and Wolf (2021), this project illustrates that LPs and VARs yield the same impulse responses under standard identification strategies.

## Key aspects:
- Comparison of LPs and VARs using U.S. macroeconomic data (1984–2024)  
- Replication of three classic identification strategies:  
  - Recursive (Christiano, Eichenbaum, Evans, 2005)  
  - Long-run (Blanchard & Quah, 1989)  
  - Sign (Uhlig, 2005)  
- Data source: FRED-QD (McCracken & Ng, 2021)  

The goal is to verify the theoretical equivalence of LP- and VAR-based impulse responses, abstracting from issues of inference and interpretation.
